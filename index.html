Automix

Joins songs into one file: trims silence, evens out loudness and crossfades between tracks. Everything runs in your browser and files are never uploaded (except a 30-second clip sent to Gemini if you add a key).

Settings

Gemini is optional: it only names genres and guesses BPM for songs added by name. Tempo, loudness and similarity are always measured from the audio itself, so Smart sort works without a key. The key is stored only in this browser.

Tracks

Drop mp3 / wav / m4a files or .zip archives with songs here, or click to choose. You can also drop files straight onto the list, at the exact spot you want

Type 3+ letters and pick a result: title, cover and genre come from iTunes, BPM from Gemini (if a key is set). Each found song gets a 30-second preview (▶). Full songs cannot be downloaded here, so to put a song into the mix, drop your own audio file onto its row.

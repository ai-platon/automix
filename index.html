Automix

Joins songs into one file: trims silence, evens out loudness and crossfades between tracks. Everything runs in your browser and files are never uploaded (except a 30-second clip sent to Gemini if you add a key).

Settings

The key is stored only in this browser. Without a key, genres are not detected and sorting uses BPM only. You can create a free key in Google AI Studio.

Tracks

Drop mp3 / wav / m4a files here or click to choose. You can also drop files straight onto the list, at the exact spot you want

The song is added as a placeholder with Gemini's guess of genre and BPM, so it takes part in sorting. Later, drop the real audio file onto its row to replace it. Placeholders are skipped when building the mix.
